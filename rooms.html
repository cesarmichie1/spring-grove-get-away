Rooms & Amenities

6 bedrooms across 3 independent units

Spring Grove Get Away is one lake house split into three privately-bookable units, one per level. Book a single unit, or reserve all three for the whole house. Every room below is confirmed straight from the property's own photos, unit by unit — anything we haven't verified yet is flagged rather than guessed at.
