One lake house, three ways to book it

Spring Grove Get Away is a three-level timber-and-stone house on Spring Grove Pond, and each level is its own independent, privately-bookable unit — its own bedrooms, its own bathroom, its own kitchen. Book a single unit for a smaller group, or reserve all three and take over the entire lake house. Every detail below is confirmed straight from the property's own photos — anything we haven't verified yet is flagged rather than guessed at.

Unit 3 · The Casino Level

A reversible poker & craps table, plus a sauna

Flip the felt and it's craps; flip it back and it's poker. Down the hall, an infrared cedar sauna and a two-person jetted soaking tub round out the top floor.

Spring Grove Pond

Right on the water, no motorized boats

Canoe, kayak, and paddleboard from the shoreline, then dry off in the hammock strung between two white pines at the water's edge — shared by whichever units are staying.
